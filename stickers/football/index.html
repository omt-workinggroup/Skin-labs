Good to know

How much are the sticker packs?

Every pack comes in two sizes: a small A5 sheet for GH₵30 and a big A4 sheet with all the stickers for GH₵60. The A3 pick & mix sheets, about 84 stickers from a whole group of categories, are GH₵100. Single stickers you pick yourself cost GH₵3 each. Custom stickers made from your own picture, name or logo start at GH₵15.

How do I order?

Tap Buy now on any pack or skin (or add a few things to your cart), pick your free sticker, add your name, WhatsApp number and location, and pay securely with Mobile Money or card on Paystack. You come straight back to your order page, where you can follow it from printing to delivery.

How do I pay?

Online with Paystack: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money or a bank card. The payment is confirmed with Paystack before your order goes to print. Delivery is not included in the online price: we agree it with you on WhatsApp.

Can I track my order?

Yes. After paying you get your own order page (it is also under Your orders on the shop). It shows each step with the time: payment confirmed, preparing artwork, printing, quality check, ready, out for delivery and delivered.
