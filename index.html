About Skin Lab

Skin Lab is a sticker shop run by Baiden Creatives in Accra, Ghana. We print laptop sticker packs on laminated vinyl: 36 ready-made sheets and 973 designs across 13 categories. A full sheet costs GH₵50 or GH₵60, single stickers are GH₵3, and custom stickers from your own picture, name or logo start at GH₵15. Every order is placed on WhatsApp and paid with MoMo.

Questions people ask

How much are the sticker packs?

A full printed sheet costs GH₵60 for packs with 25 or more stickers and GH₵50 for smaller sheets. Single stickers you pick yourself cost GH₵3 each. Custom stickers made from your own picture, name or logo start at GH₵15.

How do I order?

Choose a pack or pick single stickers on the website, add your name and location, and tap "Send order on WhatsApp". Your order arrives as one message on WhatsApp (055 836 2423). We confirm, you pay with MoMo, and we arrange delivery or pickup.

Where are you based and do you deliver?

Skin Lab is run by Baiden Creatives in Accra, Ghana. Delivery or pickup is arranged with you on WhatsApp after you order, including to university campuses.

Will the stickers peel off?

They are printed on vinyl and laminated, so they handle bags, sweaty palms and rain. Clean and dry the surface before sticking and press down firmly for a few seconds.

How big are the stickers?

Most stickers are between 3 and 6 cm, sized for a laptop lid, the area around the keyboard, a mousepad, a phone case or a water bottle.

Do you have sticker ideas I can choose from?

Yes. The Inspo page has real sticker setups. Tap any photo you like and send it on WhatsApp, and we design and print stickers in that style. Custom stickers start from GH₵15.

Do you make laptop skins?

Yes. Skin Lab prints full laptop skins as well as stickers: sticker-bomb skins made from our packs, galaxy, carbon fibre, marble and plain colours, or your own picture. Skin prices depend on your laptop, so message us on WhatsApp with your laptop model and we will tell you the price.

Can I see the stickers on my laptop before I order?

Yes. In Design, pick your laptop brand and model (Apple, HP, Dell, Lenovo, Acer, Asus, Microsoft and more), then place skins and stickers on the lid, keyboard area and bottom at real size, and send the design with your order.
