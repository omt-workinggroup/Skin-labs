    Why we exist

    Most people buy a car once every few years. The person selling it does it every week. That's not a fair fight, so we sit on your side of the table.

    We started De Car Guide after watching friends and family lose money on cars that looked perfect on the day and fell apart within a month. Rolled-back mileage. Flood damage under fresh carpet. Engines warmed up before you arrived so you'd never hear the cold start.

    None of it is hard to spot if you know where to look and you're not in a hurry. So that's what we do: we look, we take our time, and we write down what we find in plain words. If the car's good, great. If it isn't, you hear it from us first.

    How it works

    No forms. No call centre. Just a conversation.

    1.

    Send us the car

    A link, a photo of the listing, or just the make, model and where it is. That's enough to start.

    2.

    We go and look

    We book a time with the seller and inspect it in person. You can come along or stay home.

    3.

    You get the truth

    A clear report with photos, the problems we found, rough repair costs, and what we'd pay for it.

    4.

    You decide

    Buy it, haggle, or walk. If you buy, we help with the papers. If you walk, we help you find the next one.

    The checklist

    What we actually look at, every time.

    • Cold start & idle
    • Engine noise under load
    • Oil level & condition
    • Coolant & leaks
    • Exhaust smoke colour
    • Gearbox & clutch feel
    • Diagnostic scan (OBD)
    • Warning lights history
    • Brakes & discs
    • Suspension & bushes
    • Steering play
    • Tyres: wear, age, match
    • Paint depth, panel by panel
    • Panel gaps & respray signs
    • Chassis & underbody rust
    • Signs of accident repair
    • Flood & water damage
    • Airbags & seatbelts
    • AC, windows, electrics
    • Mileage vs. wear
    • Service history
    • VIN / chassis number match
    • Ownership documents
    • Test drive, our route

    This isn't a list we print to look busy. It's what we go through on every car, and you'll see each point in your report, with photos where it matters.

    Questions

    Things people usually ask.

    Do I need to be there for the inspection?

    No. Plenty of our clients are at work or in another city. We'll call you from the car if something needs talking through, and the full report comes to you on WhatsApp or email the same day.

    What if the seller won't let you inspect it?

    Then that tells you something. A seller with a good car has nothing to hide. We'd treat it as a red flag and suggest you move on.

    Can you check cars at a dealership too?

    Yes. Dealers, private sellers, cars just cleared from the port. Anywhere we can get proper access to the car.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the service and the distance to the car. Send us the details and we'll give you a fixed price before we do anything. Compared to buying the wrong car, it's very small money.

    Do you sell cars?

    No, and that's on purpose. We don't sell cars and we don't take commission from people who do. The only person paying us is you, so the only person we work for is you.
