Questions we hear a lot

How many guests can you hold?Up to 600 banquet-style in the main hall. Smaller events feel just as good — we scale the layout to fit.
Can I bring my own vendors?Yes. Decorators, DJs and photographers are welcome. Outside caterers pay a GH₵ 2,000 kitchen & service fee.
How long is the venue mine?8 hours, including setup. Extra time is GH₵ 1,500 per hour, booked in advance.
What if I need to cancel?60+ days out, we refund 75% of your deposit. Inside 60 days the deposit is kept, but you can move your date once, free, within 6 months.
Is there backup power?Yes. A standby generator keeps lights, sound and air-con running, so “lights off” never ends the party.
Can I see the space first?Please do. Viewings run daily by appointment — just send us a message.
Is parking free for guests?Yes. We have a very large on-site car park, so nobody circles the block in heels.
When can my decorator set up?From the morning of your event. Need the evening before? It’s included in VIP, or GH₵ 3,000 as an add-on.
Book earlySaturdays from October to December are usually gone by mid-year. Secure your preferred date today.
