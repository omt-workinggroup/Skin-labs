Frequently asked

How many guests can you hold?Up to 600 banquet-style. We scale the layout for smaller events.
What if I need to cancel?60+ days out, 75% of your deposit back. Inside 60 days, move your date once, free, within 6 months.
Can I bring my own vendors?Yes. Outside caterers pay a GH₵ 2,000 kitchen & service fee.
Is there backup power?Yes — a standby generator keeps everything running.
How long is the venue mine?8 hours including setup. Extra time is GH₵ 1,500 per hour.
When can my decorator set up?From the morning of. Evening-before access is in VIP, or GH₵ 3,000.
Is parking free for guests?Yes — a very large car park on site.
Can I see the space first?Please do. Viewings daily, by appointment.
