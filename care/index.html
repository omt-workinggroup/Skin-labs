Shop / Care guide

Look after your stickers.

How to put on stickers and skins so they last, and how to get help if something is not right. General guidance for vinyl: be gentle with special laptop finishes.

  1. Clean the spot

    Wipe the surface with a dry cloth or a little isopropyl alcohol, then let it dry fully. Dust and grease are the main reason stickers lift.

  2. Peel and place

    Peel the sticker from one corner. Hover it over the spot, touch down one edge first and smooth it across with your thumb so no air gets trapped.

  3. Press firmly

    Press over the whole sticker for 10 to 20 seconds, edges last. The glue gets stronger over the next day, so avoid scratching at the edges at first.

  4. Laptop skins

    Line the skin up with the laptop edges before it touches. Start from one side and smooth towards the other with a card or your palm, pushing bubbles out to the edge. A small bubble usually goes away in a day.

  5. Keeping them fresh

    Clean with a soft, slightly damp cloth. Avoid harsh cleaners and scraping. Vinyl handles rain, bags and daily use.

  6. Taking one off

    Lift a corner with a fingernail and peel slowly and low, close to the surface. A little warmth from a hair dryer on low helps. Any leftover glue wipes off with a cloth and a drop of alcohol.
